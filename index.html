THE STORY

Trade knowledge.
Project perspective.

SILK & STONE is being built on a simple idea: good tiling is about more than placing tiles. It takes preparation, sound judgement, precise execution and clear communication.

Our founder started on the tools before moving into operations and project management with a commercial flooring and tiling contractor in Canada. That experience included coordinating installation teams, materials, schedules, quality checks and client expectations.

Now based in Western Australia, the focus is on bringing that practical and professional perspective to tiling work for homeowners, builders and property managers.

GOOD TO KNOW

Common
questions.

A few practical answers before we get started.

Do you take on small tiling jobs?

Small repairs and individual tiling jobs can be discussed. Availability depends on the scope, location and schedule.

Can I send photos for an estimate?

Yes, photos and approximate measurements help clarify the work. Some projects will still need a site assessment before firm pricing.

Do you work with builders and contractors?

Builder and contractor enquiries are welcome. The founder has experience with commercial site coordination and installation teams.

Do you offer full bathroom renovations?

Not currently advertised as a standalone service. The initial focus is tiling and related finishing work. Broader renovation services may be added later once the necessary requirements are confirmed.

Which areas do you service?

The intended initial market is Perth and surrounding suburbs. Please confirm your suburb and current availability before booking.

Can you undertake waterproofing?

Waterproofing is not currently listed as an independently offered service. Any future offering will depend on the applicable requirements, insurance and verified capability.
